Our Heritage · Est. 1996

Three generations,
one family.

Established in Georgia in 1996, Dimo Fine Jewelry is a family-run business — now in its third generation of serving its neighbors across Georgia.

1996 Founded
3rd Generation
150+ Years Combined
1 Family

With more than 150 years of combined experience and expertise, it is not surprising that Dimo has been invited to be a part of countless weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, graduations, and other life-changing events.

As we learn the stories of our customers, we have the privilege of engaging in the adventure of singling out the choicest brands and designs for the perfect, essential piece for each occasion.

Just as we built our store to last from generation to generation, we work with our customers to select the best piece to last beyond their lifetime.

We look forward with great anticipation to joining in more life stories as we grow and meet new customers seeking to create lasting memories and lifetime treasures.

Only the best. Nothing less.

— The Dimo Family
